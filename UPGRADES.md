# Upgrades

## 1.0.0 — signal 7.0.0's exchange layer

A clean breaking wire deployment with no compatibility path. Nothing serves
this contract yet; a future Ethos Nexus and its meta CLI are built from this
revision together, beside signal-ethos-zero 1.0.0.

- signal-frame is gone. The contract rides signal 7.0.0 `66e7b153`: a meta
  connection is greeted once with the digest of `ethos/signal.ethos`
  (`impl signal::Contracted for Query`), a query travels as
  `Dispatch::Open` on the exchange the peer names, and responses come back
  as `Delivery::Answer` on it until `Delivery::End`. A peer built from any
  other source, the ordinary Ethos-zero contract included, is refused with
  `ContractMismatch`. ContractId 8, wire revision 4 and the route codes no
  longer exist.
- Removed with signal-frame: `MetaEthosZeroWire`, `MetaEthosZeroFrame`,
  `ExchangeDecodeFault`, `WireConversion`, `RequestWire`, `ResponseWire`,
  and the free functions `encode_request`, `encode_response`,
  `decode_request` and `decode_response` (with their private
  `request_route` and `response_route`). Framing is signal's
  `Signalizable` and `Restorable` on `Dispatch<Query>` and
  `Delivery<Response>`; a consumer that called them writes and reads those
  instead.
- Generated by ethos-zero 15.0.0 `3d330276`. The roots are `Query` and
  `Response` (were `Request` and `Response`), structs have named fields,
  and datom derives sit behind the new `datom` feature, which a meta CLI
  enables. Under 15.0.0 `Text` is undeclared (refused as
  `Undeclared.Text`), so every `Text` alias is now `String`. A bare variant
  that names a declared type now carries that type, which would have turned
  the selection `MetaObservationSelection.[ Configuration Sources ]` into
  payload-bearing variants; to keep both bare, the struct `Configuration`
  is renamed `EthosNexusConfiguration` and the vector alias `Sources` became
  an inline `Vector<Source>` in `SourceIndex` (Rust field `source_vector`).
  Datom text is unchanged by the renames.
- Repinned: datom-codec 0.32.2 `4dff16b4` and protos 0.32.2 `15b41da8`, both
  optional under `datom`; ethos-zero 15.0.0 `3d330276` at build time only.
- A one-field struct is written in braces in datom text:
  `Observed.Sources.{ [ { workspace ethos/signal.ethos } ] }`.
  `examples/round-trip.datom` is now read by a test.

## 0.5.0 — distinct global structural binding

This breaking wire change moves the privileged Ethos-zero contract from the
colliding historical Orchestrate identity to ContractId 8 while retaining its
structural wire revision 4. Contract IDs are global identities in the shared
signal-frame header; a revision is contract-local and cannot distinguish a
different component. Upgrade every Ethos-zero privileged peer together. Frames
carrying Orchestrate's 2/3 binding are rejected as foreign before archive decoding.

## 0.3.0 — generated lifecycle contract

This is a breaking wire change. Deploy every MetaEthosZero peer with
`meta-signal-ethos-zero` 0.3.0 in one coordinated rollout. The contract is now
generated from `ethos/signal.ethos`, carries interface 0.3.0 and wire revision
3, and adds Subscribe/Unsubscribe lifecycle requests, typed
`InvalidRelativePath` refusals, and configuration/source stream events.

Do not mix 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 peers on one socket. Drain or stop the old peer,
deploy the new peer and its generated contract revision, then reopen the socket.

## 0.2.0 — framed contract identity

This is a breaking wire change. Deploy every MetaEthosZero peer with
`meta-signal-ethos-zero` 0.2.0 in one coordinated rollout. New frames carry
contract identity 2 and wire revision 2 inside the rkyv envelope; 0.2.0 rejects
frames with the former revision or another contract identity before accepting their
bodies.

Do not mix 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 peers on one socket. Drain or stop the old peer, deploy
the new peer and its pinned contract revision, then reopen the socket.
